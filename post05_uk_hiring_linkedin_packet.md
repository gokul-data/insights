# Post05 — UK hiring-market signal

## LinkedIn caption

The UK hiring market is getting tighter.

ONS's latest release puts vacancies at **707,000** for May to July 2026 — down **6,000** on the previous quarter and **19,000** year on year.

The number that changes the interpretation: there are now **2.5 unemployed people per vacancy**.

That is a useful dashboard reminder: neither vacancy totals nor unemployment rates tell the whole story in isolation. The ratio between supply and demand better captures the competition people experience in the market.

Two practical implications:

• For jobseekers: a stronger application is increasingly a proof-of-impact document — make the problem, action and measurable outcome easy to find.
• For hiring teams: fewer open roles does not necessarily mean easier hiring; sharper role design and faster decisions matter when good candidates have to navigate a more selective market.

Nine of the 18 industry sectors saw vacancies fall on the quarter. The market is not closed — but it is asking for more signal from both sides.

How are you seeing the balance between open roles and candidate competition change in your field?
