# LinkedIn caption

Europe's cloud adoption has crossed 50%. But the platform layer tells a more useful leadership story.

Eurostat's 2025 enterprise data shows:

- 52.7% of EU enterprises use paid cloud services.
- PaaS reaches only 26.1% of those cloud adopters.
- Among adopters, large enterprises are 2.34× as likely as small firms to use PaaS.

That distinction matters. SaaS adoption can show access and migration; PaaS is a stronger signal that teams can build, test and deploy on a shared platform. The denominators aren't interchangeable, so I've kept them separate in the analysis.

From a business and BI perspective, I wouldn't stop at “Are we in the cloud?” I'd examine developer enablement, platform guardrails, FinOps and reusable standards by business unit. That's where migration starts becoming operating capability.

For data and technology leaders: which metric best reveals whether your cloud programme has moved from adoption to capability?
